# 🚀 SolarClient — Download & Installation Guide

Thank you for downloading **SolarClient (v3.20.8)**!

---

### 📥 How to Install

1. Download **`SolarClient-Setup-3.20.8.exe`**.
2. Double-click the installer to launch setup.
3. Choose your install location and click **Install**.

---

### 🛡️ "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen) — Why does this appear?

When installing for the first time, Windows Defender SmartScreen may display a blue warning:
> *"Windows protected your PC — Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting."*

#### **Why does this happen?**
* Microsoft displays this notification on **any newly released software** whose developers do not purchase a commercial enterprise certificate ($400+/year).
* **SolarClient is 100% free and open-source.** We do not inject telemetry, adware, or paywalls.

#### **How to run it:**
1. On the blue SmartScreen popup, click **"More info"**.
2. Click the **"Run anyway"** button that appears.
*(You only have to do this once — Windows remembers your choice!)*
